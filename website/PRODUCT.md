## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS, no build step. Published as a static site (GitHub Pages via `git subtree push`, plus a Dockerfile with nginx). Kept as is; the user did not ask for a framework.

## Users

Owners and managers of small and medium businesses (inferred from the flyers: +58 phone, SENIAT, so most likely Venezuela). They sit in gyms, clinics, academies, restaurants, lending businesses or shops, and they arrive mostly from Instagram or WhatsApp on a phone. Their job: find out whether Total System has a system for their kind of business and contact the team by email for a demo.

## Product Purpose

Total System sells software and automation to businesses: a WhatsApp chatbot, electronic invoicing, ready-made management systems per industry, and custom software. The site must make each offer findable by industry and turn the visit into an email asking for a demo. Success is a demo-request email, and organic search visibility for each service.

## Positioning

Sector-specific systems (one named system per industry, multi-branch, real time, mobile) plus custom development, sold through one direct conversation. The brand already exists as a series of Instagram flyers; the site continues that series.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services taken only from the ten flyers:
- Chatbot for WhatsApp customer service: instant automatic replies, FAQ and catalog handling.
- Electronic invoicing "conforme a la normativa del SENIAT": mobile app, send to customer, PDF download, history and reports, sales control.
- MedicPro (health): clinical histories, appointments, prescriptions and billing.
- AcademiaPro (education): enrollment, attendance and grades, one or several campuses, classes and courses, payments.
- Restaurants, cafes, gastronomy: tables, orders, kitchen and cash register, one or several branches in real time.
- Gyms, CrossFit, sports centers: shifts, memberships and charges, member status and plan expiry in real time.
- Loans and collections: interest, payment dates and client portfolio for lenders.
- Custom software ("Tú pones las reglas y nosotros ponemos el código").
- Attendance control and clock-in system (flyer added 2026-10-09), for companies and SMEs: automates working-hours registration, shift control and hours calculation, adapted to the client's needs; multi-device clock-in, automatic calculations, PDF and Excel reports. ## Evidence on Hand

Ten flyers (see `assets/servicios/`) with real product mockups, the Jarvis screenshots in `assets/screenshots/`. No testimonials, client logos, case studies, metrics or prices exist; none may be fabricated. UI values inside the flyers (e.g. "$420", "1.240 alumnos") are illustrative mockup data.

## Product Principles

- Industry first: a visitor must see their own business type within seconds.
- One action: write to the contact email, always reachable, with a prefilled subject for the service they were reading about.
- Only claim what the flyers claim; no invented prices, clients or timelines.
- Every service is plain indexable text, never only an image.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Spanish-language audience on mid-range Android phones and slow connections: light pages, self-hosted fonts, readable contrast, keyboard and screen-reader friendly.
